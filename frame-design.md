# Frame Design and Mechanical Modifications

## Overview

Several mechanical modifications were made during the project.

The original configuration did not provide sufficient space for all additional components, especially the LiDAR, Raspberry Pi, camera and servo.

The mechanical design was therefore developed iteratively.

---

# 1. 3D-Printed Components

The documented components include:

- Raspberry Pi mounting plate,
- landing feet Version 1,
- landing feet Version 2,
- larger cage prototype,
- camera case,
- additional mounting components.

---

# 2. Larger Cage Prototype

A larger protective cage was tested.

The configuration was not suitable for the final component arrangement.

One important limitation was the additional space required for the servo.

The tested cage was therefore not retained in this form.

---

# 3. Raspberry Pi Mount

A dedicated mounting plate was designed for the Raspberry Pi.

This provided a dedicated mounting solution for the additional onboard computer.

---

# 4. Landing Feet Version 1

The first version of the landing feet was 3D printed.

Foam/pool-noodle material was added as damping.

The objective was to reduce vibration and landing impacts.

However, the first version did not provide enough clearance underneath the drone for the servo.

---

# 5. Landing Feet Version 2

A second, longer version was developed.

The main reason was the additional space required for the servo.

The second version again included damping material.

---

# 6. Vibration Damping

Vibration was a recurring mechanical problem.

Damping was important not only for landing but also because vibration could affect the LiDAR attachment and general flight stability.

---

# 7. Camera Case

A dedicated camera case was 3D printed and attached to the drone.

This provided a dedicated mounting and protection solution for the camera.

---

# 8. Servo Clearance

The servo created an additional mechanical requirement below the drone.

The landing gear therefore had to be modified to provide sufficient clearance.

---

# 9. Iterative Development

The mechanical development can be summarized as:

```text
Additional components required
            ↓
3D-printed components
            ↓
Larger cage tested
            ↓
Servo space problem identified
            ↓
Landing Feet Version 1
            ↓
Additional clearance required
            ↓
Landing Feet Version 2
            ↓
Longer feet + vibration damping
```

---

# 10. Current Status

Several mechanical modifications have been implemented and tested.

The design remains iterative because the final arrangement depends on the remaining sensor, Raspberry Pi and delivery-system integration.

Further changes may therefore be required.
